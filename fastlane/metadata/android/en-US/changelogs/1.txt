First release.

- Search for a site and browse its Wayback Machine history on a
  year / month / day heat calendar.
- Per-day snapshot list with hour / minute / second filters.
- Star favourite sites and snapshots; local History of opened links.
- Share a link into the app to open its archive calendar.
- FOSS-first search (Wikidata, Wikipedia, Marginalia) + DuckDuckGo, HN, npm.
- Light / dark / system theme, reorderable home tabs.
- Fully client-side: no account, no backend, no tracking.
