WayWayBack is a small, fast, fully client-side browser for the Internet Archive
Wayback Machine.

Search for a website, then walk its history on a heat-map calendar: pick a year,
a month and a day and see exactly how many times it was captured. Open any
snapshot in your browser, star the sites and captures you care about, and keep a
local history of what you have looked at.

Features

* Year -> month -> day calendar with capture counts and a heat scale
* Years, months and days with no captures are marked and never re-fetched
* Fast: uses the Wayback Machine's own calendar endpoints, and caches locally
* Per-day snapshot list with smart hour / minute / second filters
* Search merges instant URL guesses with optional Wikipedia and npm
  lookups; recent searches are remembered
* Star favourite sites and snapshots; History of everything you opened
* Share a link into the app to jump straight to its archive calendar
* Light / dark / system theme, reorderable home tabs
* No account, no backend, no tracking, no ads. All data stays on your device.

WayWayBack is an independent, unofficial client. It is not the Wayback Machine
and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Internet Archive. Archived content
and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
